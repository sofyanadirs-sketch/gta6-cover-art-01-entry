Cover Studio turns your own photos into bold GTA-style multi-panel cover art: crop and position each photo per panel, fill several panels with one photo or several, write the caption in heavy poster lettering, pick a Vice City sunset or neon colour grade, and export a share-ready PNG in 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 or 1920 × 1080. Free, runs fully offline in desktop Chrome, and your photos never leave your machine — no uploads, no Rockstar logos, artwork or fonts.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — pick a layout, upload your photos (or use the bundled samples), drag to crop, type your caption, and hit Download PNG cover.
